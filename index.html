About Pambana

Turning surplus fruits into something special.

Pambana Enterprise is a Zambian food business turning locally sourced fruit into naturally dried snacks. We help transform surplus fruit into delicious products while creating value for local farmers.

Our products are made in Zambia from real fruit, with no artificial colours or preservatives. We believe good fruit can be enjoyed well beyond its harvest season.

We are proud to support local communities and reduce food waste by turning surplus fruits into naturally dried snacks.

Our Purpose

Good for people. Good for the planet.

We believe that what would otherwise go to waste can become something valuable. Our work is about more than snacks — it is about building a sustainable and inclusive food system.

Reducing Food Waste

We help preserve surplus fruits that might otherwise go to waste, extending their shelf life through dehydration.

Supporting Farmers

Through farmer partnerships and value addition, we aim to create new market opportunities for smallholder farmers.

Protecting Our Planet

Our solar and energy-efficient drying technologies support more sustainable food preservation practices.
